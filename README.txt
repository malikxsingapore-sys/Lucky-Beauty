LUCKY BEAUTY STORE
==================
A responsive original wig-store starter inspired by the shopping structure of the reference store.

Files:
- index.html — complete storefront demo.

Payments:
The demo intentionally does NOT collect card numbers or PayPal credentials. For live payments, connect a legitimate payment provider/merchant account and replace the checkout with its hosted/official checkout flow.

Customize:
- Product names/prices are in the Featured Wigs section of index.html.
- Replace the placeholder product panels with your own product images.
